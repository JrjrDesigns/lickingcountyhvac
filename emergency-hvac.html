24/7 Emergency HVAC Service in Licking County, Ohio

Heating and cooling emergencies don't follow business hours. Our technicians are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week — including weekends and holidays — for emergency HVAC service throughout Licking County. We cover Newark, Heath, Granville, Pataskala, Utica, Buckeye Lake, Hebron, Johnstown, and New Albany.

If your furnace goes out on a cold January night or your AC fails during a July heat wave, we treat it as the emergency it is. We prioritize same-night and same-day response for all emergency calls and will give you a clear repair estimate before any work begins — even at 2am.

Signs You Need Emergency HVAC

  • No heat when outdoor temperatures are below freezing
  • No cooling during extreme heat conditions
  • Gas smell near furnace or HVAC equipment
  • Carbon monoxide detector triggered
  • Complete system failure
  • Water flooding from air handler
  • Electrical burning smell from HVAC equipment
  • Elderly, infant, or medically vulnerable household members affected
