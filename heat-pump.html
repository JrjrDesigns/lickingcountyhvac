Heat Pump Repair & Installation in Licking County, Ohio

Heat pumps are increasingly common in Ohio homes as an efficient alternative to traditional furnace and AC systems. We install, repair, and maintain all major heat pump brands throughout Licking County — serving Newark, Heath, Granville, Pataskala, Utica, Buckeye Lake, Hebron, Johnstown, and New Albany.

Heat pumps require different expertise than standard HVAC systems — particularly in Ohio winters when supplemental heating comes into play. Our technicians are trained on both standard and cold-climate heat pumps and can help you determine whether a heat pump is the right choice for your Licking County home.

Signs You Need Heat Pump Service

  • Heat pump blowing cool air in heating mode
  • Unit iced over in winter or summer
  • Auxiliary heat running constantly
  • System short-cycling
  • Strange noises from outdoor unit
  • Higher than expected electric bills
  • Heat pump not reaching set temperature
  • Interested in upgrading to a heat pump system
